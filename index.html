How it works

Step 1

Describe or record

Point Qure at a feature: a task, a spec or your code. Or record the flow once in its built-in browser.

Step 2

Review what it will test

Qure proposes scenarios, runs them, and shows recordings and any issues found, so you check the behaviour, not just the code.

Step 3

Keep the tests

Deterministic Playwright or Cypress tests land in your repository, ready for your normal workflow.
